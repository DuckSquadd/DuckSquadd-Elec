1. Introduction

DuckSquadd values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and
protect information when you play our game "Elec".

2. Data Collection

When creating an account to play "Elec", we collect the following information:

	: Password
	: Email

All account information is securely stored and encrypted, Passwords are hashed and
are never stored in plain text.

3. Information We Collect

We collect data on how you interact with Elec, including:

	: Chat Logs
	: In-Game Messages
	: Crash Reports.

This information helps us maintain game functionality, enforce rules, and improve the
player experience.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. How We Use Information

Information collected during account creation and gameplay is used to:
	: Respond to inquiries and provide support
	: Verify and manage accounts
	: Enforce rules and restrict accounts when necessary
	: Maintain game security
	: Comply with applicable laws and regulations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Sharing of Information

DuckSquadd does not share your information with third parties. Information is only
disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect our rights or the safety of our
players.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. Data Retention

We retain your information only while your account remains active and as needed to
fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy.

Once an account is deleted, all associated data is permanently removed from our
servers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. Security

DISCLAIMER:
	No system is entirely immune to external interference or malicious activity.
	DuckSquadd implements essential and up-to-date security measures to protect
	your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely foolproof.

All data sent to and from DuckSquadd servers is encrypted during transmission. Data
stored on our servers is hashed, encrypted, and never stored in plain text.

Elec is not intended for children under the age of 13.

If an account is discovered to belong to a child under the age of 13, the account will be
terminated immediately and all associated data will be removed from our servers.
